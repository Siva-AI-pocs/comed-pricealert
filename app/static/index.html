My ComEd Usage Data

Upload a Green Button download from your ComEd account to chart your own usage alongside prices.

How to get your file:
  1. Log in to ComEd → My Usage Data
  2. Choose Green Button Download and pick the date range you want (up to 13 months)
  3. Save the resulting .xml or .zip file
  4. Upload it below — re-uploads are safe (existing intervals are updated, not duplicated)
